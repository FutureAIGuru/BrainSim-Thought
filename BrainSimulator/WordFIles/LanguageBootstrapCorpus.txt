# Minimal language-learning corpus
# Format: phrase [grounded exemplar]
# The semantic endpoint uses the singular concept even when the surface phrase is plural.

Cats are animals. [cat->!.is-a->animal]
Horses are animals. [horse->!.is-a->animal]
Birds are animals. [bird->!.is-a->animal]

Dogs have tails. [dog->!.has->tail]

Birds have wings. [bird->!.has->wing]

Dogs can bark. [dog->!.can->bark]

Birds can fly. [bird->!.can->fly]
